About

The Alternative Process Lab is a browser darkroom. It takes the photographic processes of the nineteenth century, the chemistry, the waiting, the small failures, and rebuilds them as light and mathematics so that anyone with a phone can print the way the first photographers printed.

Each simulation follows the real workflow of its process: the negative, the coating, the exposure, the wash. The results are honest to the chemistry, including the ways a print can go wrong.

Privacy

Every step runs on your own device. Photographs are never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone else. The downloaded print is rendered locally and carries no hidden data from the original file.
